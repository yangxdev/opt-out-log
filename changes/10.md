## Change

- opt-out-log moves from the `page` layout to the `app` layout. It is one screen, `/`, and the checklist is now the first thing under the bar: no hero, no stat cells, no numbered `Section`s, no "How it works" section.
- `src/App.tsx` is rebuilt from `AppShell` with an `AppHeader` (no tabs; the theme switch stays) and an `AppFooter` carrying the existing note "This lists switches only. It cannot read your settings and stores nothing outside this browser." and the links "Source" and "Suggest a change".
- One `ViewHeader`: title "Checklist" (the h1; the old headline goes), meta = the existing summary line "N of T checked · M to re-check", action = **Copy my checklist** (still the one primary button), toolbar = the platform filter.
- Under the header, on `px-edge`: the one-line explanation "Tick each switch once you have turned it off. Anything not re-checked in 30 days is flagged." and, while any entry has `verifiedOn === null`, the unverified notice, then the ruled list (or the empty state) edge to edge.
- Stays exactly as it is: every feature and its behaviour (ticking, the 30-day stale rule, "Re-check due", "Not verified yet", filter counts, Copy my checklist copying the full list regardless of the filter, its clipboard fallback and textarea, the empty state), `src/data/settings.json`, the `localStorage` key `opt-out-log:checks:v1`, `GET /api/health`, both themes, no horizontal scroll at 320px.
